To improve query performance in case billions of records:
- Use partitioning strategy like partitioning order by date or hour to reduce amount of scanned data
- Use indexing on primary keys or frequently selected/filtered columns like which may filter parts of the data that weren't included in conditioned queries like completed orders query, 
date-interval queries, etc ...
- Optimize data types to reduce size of scanned columns
- Avoid full scans (select *)
